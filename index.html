AI Retained Chat Simulation

You've invested time in learning, practicing, and developing the skills needed for meaningful client conversations. Now it's time for the final step of your qualification journey.

This module is designed to assess your ability to create the kind of experience that keeps clients engaged, connected, and wanting to continue the conversation. Success isn't measured by speed or quick answers — it's measured by your ability to build trust, provide value, and maintain a natural flow throughout the session.

To preserve the authenticity of the experience, this challenge is available only once. Approach it with confidence, trust your preparation, and allow your skills to speak for themselves.

How it works +

You'll meet one client — Michelle. Before the chat begins, you'll receive a short summary about her, including her background, emotional state, and opening message. Take a moment to review the information and consider how you'd like to approach the conversation.

There is no time limit for this session. Instead, a timer will track the total duration of your chat. The objective is not simply to make the conversation longer, but to create enough value, curiosity, and connection that Michelle naturally wants to stay engaged.

Every response is an opportunity to deepen trust, uncover new layers of the client's situation, and guide the conversation forward in a meaningful way.

This qualification can only be completed once, so make the most of the opportunity to demonstrate your ability to create a client experience that feels both supportive and compelling.

Signals of Strong Performance +

💜 Empathy
Do you recognize and respond to Michelle's emotions with care and specificity? Strong advisors go beyond generic reassurance and demonstrate a genuine understanding of the client's unique feelings, concerns, and experiences.

🔗 Engagement
Do you create opportunities for the conversation to grow? Strong engagement comes from thoughtful follow-up questions, exploring emotional nuances, and helping the client uncover deeper layers of their situation rather than focusing only on the initial question.

🎤 Tone of Voice
Does your communication feel warm, confident, and natural? Clients are more likely to remain engaged when they feel they are speaking with someone who is present, supportive, and genuinely invested in their experience.

⏱ Time Fillers
Do you maintain an active presence throughout the session? Strong advisors communicate their process, set expectations when they need a moment to reflect, and avoid unexplained silence. Keeping the client informed helps maintain trust and momentum throughout the conversation.

🔮 Reading Depth
Do your insights feel thoughtful, detailed, and meaningful? Strong readings go beyond surface-level observations to uncover patterns, emotions, motivations, and possibilities that encourage further exploration and discussion.

You've come a long way to get here. Trust yourself, do your best, and remember — we believe in you. Good luck! 🌟
